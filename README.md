# DL-Revisited

#### Neither framework is universally "better," as the choice depends entirely on your goals.

1. PyTorch dominates AI research and rapid prototyping, while TensorFlow excels in enterprise-scale production and edge deployment.

2. PyTorch (developed by Meta) has become the de facto standard (dominant choice via widespread practical use) for academia and open-source models (like Hugging Face) due to its highly flexible, Pythonic design. Conversely, TensorFlow (developed by Google) remains a massive force in commercial industries that require rigid, scalable, highly optimized production pipelines and robust mobile or web deployment.
